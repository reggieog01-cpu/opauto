What is Blur Auto Clicker?

Blur Auto Clicker is a Windows utility that automates mouse clicks at a configurable interval. It is closed-source freeware, meaning you cannot inspect its code. OP Auto Clicker achieves the same ceiling but with full source code on GitHub, VirusTotal verification on every release, and an MIT licence.
