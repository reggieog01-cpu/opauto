What is a click test?

A click test is a browser-based tool that measures how many times you can click your mouse button in a fixed time window — typically 1, 5, 10, 30, or 60 seconds. The test counts each mousedown event, divides by the elapsed seconds, and reports your clicks per second (CPS). Results are instant and show you where you fall on the click-speed distribution. Average untrained users score 4–7 CPS. Practiced regular clickers reach 8–10 CPS. Trained jitter clickers sustain 10–14 CPS. Butterfly and drag clicking exceed 20 CPS in short bursts. Click tests are popular with competitive Minecraft PvP players (Kohi 5-second test standard), Roblox clicker sim players, and idle-game enthusiasts. How to click faster on a click test

  1. 1

    Warm up your fingers

    Stretch and shake your hand for 30 seconds before the test. Cold muscles click slower.

  2. 2

    Use claw grip

    Rest your wrist on the desk and curl your finger over the button. Reduces travel distance per click.

  3. 3

    Try jitter clicking

    Tense your forearm to vibrate your finger. Bumps most users from 8 CPS to 12–14 CPS with practice.

  4. 4

    Practice daily

    Five 10-second sessions per day. Most learners gain 2–3 CPS in the first month.
