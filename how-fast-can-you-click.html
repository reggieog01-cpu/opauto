How fast can a human click?

The average adult clicks at 4–7 CPS in a 5–10 second test. This is the benchmark for untrained regular users. With technique, speeds increase significantly: jitter clicking (forearm tension vibration) reaches 10–14 CPS; butterfly clicking (two-finger alternation) reaches 15–22 CPS; drag clicking (mouse-button friction) registers 25–35+ CPS in short bursts. These techniques have practical ceilings — drag clicking wears switches fast and is banned on most competitive Minecraft servers. The absolute human ceiling in real-world testing is around 17 CPS sustained (jitter) or 22 CPS burst (butterfly).
