What is a click speed test?

A click speed test is a browser tool that counts how many times you click your mouse in a fixed duration, then reports your rate as clicks per second (CPS). Standard durations are 1, 5, 10, 30, and 60 seconds. Different durations measure different skills: short tests (1–5 s) reward peak burst speed; longer tests (30–60 s) reward endurance and rule out unsustainable techniques. The 5-second Kohi test became the de-facto standard in competitive Minecraft PvP because it matches typical combat exchange length. The 10-second test is the general competition standard used on cpstest.org and similar sites. Average untrained users score 4–7 CPS. Practiced regular clickers reach 8–10 CPS. Jitter clickers sustain 10–14 CPS. Butterfly clickers reach 15–22 CPS in bursts. Beyond 25 CPS sustained is nearly always software-assisted (auto clicker). Tips

How to improve your click speed

⏰

Warm up 30 seconds

Stretch fingers, shake hand, do 10 light warm-up clicks. Cold hands click 20% slower.

👍

Use claw grip

Wrist rests on desk, finger curled tight over the button. Reduces travel per click.

⚡

Learn jitter clicking

Forearm tension vibrates the finger. Bumps most users from 8 to 12–14 CPS in 2–4 weeks.

🕑

Practice consistently

Three 10-second sessions per day. Track your best score weekly.
