Ban risk

Will Roblox ban you for using an auto clicker?

Roblox's Byfron/Hyperion anti-cheat (introduced 2023) can detect auto-clicker patterns, but enforcement varies by game and pattern. Most clicker simulators explicitly permit auto clickers in their game rules — some even sell auto-click gamepasses. Roblox itself doesn't ban you for auto-clicking per se; risk arises when: (1) a game's developer flags your account for exploiting reward systems, (2) Byfron detects the auto-clicker binary in memory, or (3) your CPS pattern is impossibly consistent for weeks. Practical safety: use auto clickers for 1–2 hour sessions with breaks, keep CPS in the game's expected human range (30–40 for clicker sims), and don't auto-farm during official events with anti-exploit monitoring active.
