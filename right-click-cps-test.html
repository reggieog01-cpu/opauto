What is a right click CPS test?

A right click CPS test measures how many right-clicks per second you can produce in a fixed time window — typically 5 or 10 seconds. The test captures every right-click event in the browser and divides by elapsed time to give your CPS (clicks per second). Right-click speed is lower than left-click speed for nearly all users because the ring or middle finger (which controls the right button) is weaker and less conditioned than the index finger. Average right-click CPS is 3–5, compared to 4–7 for left-click. In gaming, right-click CPS matters mainly in Minecraft PvP (right-click blocking) and some clicker games. OP Auto Clicker supports right-click automation at the same 100+ CPS ceiling as left-click, using the Windows SendInput API.
