How does Jagex detect auto clickers in OSRS and RS3?

Old School RuneScape and RuneScape 3 are designed around long AFK sessions for many skills, but Jagex’s macro detection actively flags accounts that show non-human input patterns. The detection system analyses click timing regularity, interval variance, and action sequences. A perfectly uniform auto clicker firing at identical millisecond intervals is the clearest detection signal — no human clicks at exactly the same interval 500 times in a row. OP Auto Clicker’s full 100 CPS speed is never appropriate for Runescape: it exceeds any legitimate action rate and leaves a trivially detectable pattern. The recommended setup is 1–3 CPS with a randomised ±20% interval, limited to skills the game is inherently designed for AFK play. OSRS enforces stricter rules than RS3 and has a larger community of players who report suspected botters. RS3’s more complex skill actions make purely mechanical auto clicking less effective anyway. Neither game’s Terms of Service permits macro use, and a permanent ban on the account is the standard penalty.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Is using an auto clicker in Runescape bannable?

Yes. Both OSRS and RS3 Terms of Service prohibit all macros and auto clickers. Jagex issues permanent bans for confirmed macro use. The risk varies by skill and CPS — high-frequency uniform clicking is detected fastest.

What CPS is safe in OSRS?

No CPS is officially “safe” since all macro use violates ToS. Pragmatically, 1–2 CPS with randomised intervals on AFK-designed skills (woodcutting, fishing, mining) carries the lowest detection risk because it mirrors natural human behaviour.

Does Jagex detect OP Auto Clicker?

Jagex detects patterns, not specific software. A uniform 100 CPS pattern is trivially detectable. A 1–2 CPS pattern with variance is much harder to distinguish from a player who clicks slowly and consistently. The software matters far less than the pattern.

Can I AFK fish or woodcut with an auto clicker?

Fishing and woodcutting are the most AFK-friendly skills — the game expects long gaps between actions as catches and chops complete. Setting 1–2 CPS with interval randomisation is the lowest-risk setup for these skills.

What is the penalty for botting in Runescape?

Jagex’s standard penalty for confirmed macro use is a permanent ban. First offences on accounts with no prior history may receive a 2-day mute or temporary ban, but repeat or flagrant botting results in a permanent account ban with no appeal.
