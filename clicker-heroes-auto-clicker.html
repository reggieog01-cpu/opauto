What's the best auto clicker for Clicker Heroes?

OP Auto Clicker is the most popular free auto clicker for Clicker Heroes on both the Steam version and the original browser version at clickerheroes.com. The optimal setup is: Click type Left, interval 10 ms (100 CPS), Fixed position on the monster sprite, F6 hotkey. Click damage in Clicker Heroes saturates around 100 CPS — the game's animation pipeline can't visually process more, and damage events are capped at this rate too. Higher CPS wastes CPU without helping. Clicker Heroes has no anti-cheat, so all achievements (including Big Spender, Clicker Hero, and the Mercenary recruits) trigger normally. The only achievements that explicitly require manual clicking are early-game tutorial achievements, which take seconds to complete by hand. For sustained progression — especially during the early ancient soul farming phase — OP Auto Clicker is the difference between hours and days of progress.

How do you set up OP Auto Clicker for Clicker Heroes?

  1. 1

    Launch Clicker Heroes

    Open the Steam version in windowed or borderless mode, or the browser version at clickerheroes.com. Not exclusive fullscreen.

  2. 2

    Open OP Auto Clicker

    Run the 512 KB .exe. Set Click type: Left, Single.

  3. 3

    Set the interval

    Type 10 ms (= 100 CPS) for active progression. Type 50 ms (= 20 CPS) for idle ancient farming.

  4. 4

    Pick the monster position

    Click Pick location, then click on the monster sprite in Clicker Heroes. The clicker locks to that pixel.

  5. 5

    Press F6

    Tab to Clicker Heroes and press F6. Monsters die. Pause with F6 again to buy upgrades or activate Energize.

Pro tips

Clicker Heroes auto clicker tips from veterans

🎯

Pause for Energize

Activate Energize manually before re-enabling auto clicking. Energize doubles click damage for 30 seconds — the boost stacks with sustained CPS for massive burst.

⚡

Use Power Surge wisely

Power Surge multiplies damage further. Manually trigger it, then unpause OP Auto Clicker for the 30-second window.

🏆

Ascension reset workflow

Just before ascension: 1) Stop OP Auto Clicker. 2) Click manually through the ascension confirm dialog. 3) Re-enable auto clicker for the new run. Don't let auto clicker confirm dialogs accidentally.

📊

Lower CPS for late game

Past zone 2000, hero DPS is millions of times higher than click damage. Drop OP Auto Clicker to 50 ms (20 CPS) — saves CPU without slowing progression.

🎪

Position locked to map?

If you switch maps (zone transitions auto-progress), the monster sprite moves slightly. Either Pick location again per zone, or use cursor-follow mode and click in the center of the screen.

🌏

Overnight farming

Disable Windows Sleep (Settings → System → Power), set 50 ms interval, F6, walk away. Saves about 100 zones of progression overnight on a stable account.
