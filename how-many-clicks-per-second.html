How many CPS can a human do on average?

An average adult clicks 4–7 times per second with normal one-finger regular clicking on a 10-second test. Untrained users cluster around 5 CPS. Practiced gamers reach 8–10 CPS. Beyond that, specialized techniques push the ceiling: jitter clicking (10–14 CPS) uses forearm tension to vibrate the finger; butterfly clicking (15–22 CPS) alternates two fingers; drag clicking (25–35+ CPS) uses friction between the finger and a textured mouse button to trigger multiple actuations per swipe. All techniques share physical limits — the fastest sustained human CPS ever verified is around 16–17 CPS with regular clicking. Physical limits

What's the theoretical maximum CPS a human can hit?

Muscle-fiber twitch rate caps sustained one-finger clicking around 15–17 CPS. Two-finger butterfly clicking doubles the effective rate to 25 CPS. Drag clicking bypasses muscle limits entirely by using friction to trigger multiple switch actuations per finger swipe — peaks around 35 CPS are documented but degrade rapidly with duration. Anything above 35 CPS sustained for more than 5 seconds is almost certainly software-assisted.
