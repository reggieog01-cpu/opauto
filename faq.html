Setup & Usage

How to Use It

Download the .exe file, double-click to open it, set your click interval in the time fields, choose which mouse button to simulate, assign a hotkey, then switch to the target window and press your hotkey to start. Press it again to stop. No installation required — full install guide walks through it visually.
The default toggle hotkey is F6. You can change it to any key in the hotkey settings inside the app. Just make sure the key you pick doesn't conflict with a shortcut in your game or application.
OP Auto Clicker has four separate input fields: hours, minutes, seconds, and milliseconds. Set the values to control how often it clicks. For example: 0h 0m 0s 100ms = 10 clicks per second. For very fast clicking, set it to 0h 0m 0s 10ms = ~100 clicks per second.
Yes. Under the "Click Repeat" section, switch from "Repeat Indefinitely" to "Repeat (times)" and enter your desired count. The auto clicker will stop automatically after reaching that number.
Yes. In the "Click Position" settings, choose "Pick Location" and then click the spot on your screen you want to target. The app will lock clicks to those coordinates rather than following your cursor. This is useful for clicking the same button repeatedly.
The minimum interval is 1 millisecond, which is theoretically 1,000 clicks per second. In practice, your hardware and the target application impose real limits. Most users find 10–100 ms (10–100 CPS) to be the practical range. Going below 10ms rarely produces faster actual results.

Gaming

Minecraft, Roblox & More

For Minecraft (Java or Bedrock edition), a click interval of 50–100 ms (10–20 clicks per second) is a solid starting point for most tasks like mining, farming, AFK fishing, or PvP practice. If you're on a single-player world or a server that allows it, you can experiment with faster speeds. Avoid extremely fast clicking on anti-cheat protected servers like Hypixel. See the Minecraft use case overview.
OP Auto Clicker works with Roblox and is commonly used for idle games, farming simulators, and repetitive tasks. However, Roblox's terms of service prohibit cheating and some games have anti-cheat systems. For games where automation is against the rules, use it at your own risk. It's perfectly fine for offline or non-competitive use.
It depends entirely on the game. Competitive games with anti-cheat (like Valorant, CS2, or some Minecraft servers) may detect and act on unusual click patterns. Idle games, farming games, and single-player games generally have no such restrictions. Always read the game's rules before using automation tools in multiplayer environments.
