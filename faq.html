Getting Started

It's a free, lightweight auto-clicking program for Windows. The app automates mouse clicks at custom intervals, supports left/right/middle clicks, hotkey toggles, and fixed-position clicking — used by gamers, testers, and accessibility users.

Visit the download page, click the green Download button, and run the .exe. The file is 512 KB and requires no installation.

Open the app, set your preferred click interval, choose mouse button (left/right/middle) and single or double click, set a hotkey (default F6), then press the hotkey to start clicking.

The click interval controls how much time passes between each click. The app accepts intervals from 1 millisecond up to multiple hours, in milliseconds, seconds, minutes, or hours.

Press your assigned hotkey (default F6) to toggle clicking on and off. The hotkey works globally — even when the app is minimized or another window has focus.
