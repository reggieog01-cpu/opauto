What CPS do you need for Minecraft PvP?

Minecraft servers tick at 20 Hz — the server processes player input 20 times per second. Any CPS above 20 sends clicks the server discards. In practice, the useful CPS ceiling for Minecraft PvP is 15–18 CPS accounting for network latency and packet batching. Casual PvP servers reward 8–10 CPS — enough to out-click most casual opponents without triggering anti-cheat. Competitive servers (Hypixel Bedwars, Duels, Skywars) reward 12–14 CPS jitter clicking. The old Kohi PvP server standard was 14–16 CPS in 5-second combat exchanges. Butterfly clicking (15–22 CPS) and drag clicking (25–35+ CPS) are banned by Watchdog on Hypixel and most major networks because the click-interval patterns are statistically detectable. Auto clickers at 100+ CPS are banned everywhere — anti-cheat detects the consistent-interval pattern within seconds.

Why 20 CPS is the ceiling

Why higher CPS stops helping past 20

Minecraft's server tick rate is 20 Hz (20 ticks per second). All hit registration, damage calculation, and combat resolution happens on tick boundaries. If you click 40 times per second, the server sees 20 clicks per second — the other 20 are dropped. Combat exchanges are also capped by hit cooldown (introduced in 1.9) which limits damage-per-hit if you click too fast. On 1.8 PvP servers (Hypixel, Bedwars) the old system rewards raw CPS. On 1.9+ modern PvP, the sweet spot drops to 6–10 CPS to allow full charge attacks. Read the Hypixel click policy for the current allowed techniques.

15-20 CPS butterfly pattern

Watchdog detects the two-beat alternating interval characteristic of butterfly clicking. Bans start with 30-day temp-bans.

🚫

25+ CPS drag pattern

Multi-actuation bursts are instantly detected. Ban is 30-day first offense, permanent for repeat.

🔴

Consistent-interval auto clicker
