  2. 2

    Open OP Auto Clicker

    Run the 512 KB .exe. No installer.

Pro tips

Cookie Clicker auto clicker tips from idle veterans

The stuff that takes hundreds of hours to figure out, condensed.

🍪

Stack Click frenzy + Frenzy + Building special

If you catch a Click frenzy during a regular Frenzy with a Cursed Finger or Building special active, you can hit a 100,000× click multiplier. OP Auto Clicker at 1 ms during this combo is worth more than a week of normal play.

🎯

Pick location once, never re-pick

The Big Cookie always renders at the same screen pixel as long as you don't resize the window. Set it once, leave OP Auto Clicker running through dozens of sessions.

☁

Steam Cloud syncs with auto-click on

The Steam version saves to the cloud every minute. OP Auto Clicker doesn't interfere — your cookie progress saves correctly even with 1000 CPS hammering the screen.

⚙

Pause for sugar lumps

Sugar lumps spawn over a 23-hour timer and need a manual click to harvest. Press F6 to pause, harvest the lump, F6 again to resume. Don't lose a Bifurcated lump because the auto clicker hit it during ripening.

💬

The browser version saves locally

If you play at orteil.dashnet.org/cookieclicker, your save is in browser localStorage. Closing the tab loses progress — export your save (Options → Export save) before any 1000-CPS session, just in case.

🏆

Combo with the in-game CpS

OP Auto Clicker boosts click CpS, not the passive building CpS. If you're playing pure idle, your auto clicker is decoration. The real wins are during the first hour and during every Click frenzy.
