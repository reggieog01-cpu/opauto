Auto Clicker Not Detected? Here's the Truth

Every auto-clicker product claims '100% undetectable'. Almost none of them are. The honest reality: detectability depends entirely on which game you're playing. Single-player Minecraft and Cookie Clicker have zero detection. Valorant's Vanguard, Apex's EAC, and Rust's BattlEye detect every synthetic click. Here's the complete list of which games have anti-cheat, what kind, and how OP Auto Clicker fits each one.

Can auto clickers be detected by anti-cheat?

Yes — modern anti-cheat systems can detect auto clickers in two ways. Kernel-level anti-cheats (Vanguard for Valorant, Easy Anti-Cheat for Apex Legends and Fortnite, BattlEye for Rust and PUBG) inspect the source of input events at the OS level — they can tell whether a click came from your physical mouse driver or from a synthetic source like the Win32 SendInput API. Server-side anti-cheats (Hypixel, Mineplex, many MMO servers) analyze click intervals over time — perfectly consistent 100ms intervals are a giveaway that no human can produce. Tools advertised as '100% undetectable' typically just mean they bypass simple userspace checks — not the actual anti-cheat layers. The only safe rule: assume detection on competitive games, don't bother on single-player or casual servers.

How they actually detect

The 4 ways anti-cheat catches auto clickers

🔐

1. Kernel input source inspection

Vanguard, EAC, and BattlEye run as kernel drivers and can see whether mouse events came from the physical mouse driver or from a synthetic source like SendInput. No user-space tool can spoof this without compromising the kernel itself.

⏱

2. Interval consistency analysis

Server-side anti-cheats log click timestamps. Humans have natural ~10ms jitter between clicks. OP Auto Clicker at "100ms interval" produces 100.0ms intervals every time — a statistical impossibility for humans. Hypixel uses this method.

📊

3. Click-rate impossibility

Sustained 30+ CPS over minutes is humanly impossible. Even drag clicking can only burst 30 CPS for a few seconds before the technique breaks down. Auto clickers that maintain 30 CPS for 10 minutes straight get flagged on the rate alone.

🔎

4. Process scanning

Some anti-cheats maintain a blocklist of known auto-clicker process names and check if any are running while the game launches. Renaming the .exe defeats this trivially — but if you're at the renaming stage, you're in violation territory anyway.
